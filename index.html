How it works

  1. Apply with your details and the area you will cover.
  2. Hairlux reviews your application. Once approved, accept the agent terms.
  3. Share your code, link or QR code. Customers sign up with it and book.
  4. Track your results every day and withdraw to your bank account.

What counts

  • Customers who sign up with your code, link or QR code and verify their email
  • Each customer's first paid booking
  • Leads you log: if they sign up later with the same phone number, they count for you
  • Cancelled or duplicate bookings never count

What you need to apply
